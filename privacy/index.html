Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Pausivo is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that helps you ride out urges with short tasks. This policy says what the app stores, why, who else is involved, and how you delete it. The short version: we store what the app needs to work, we show no ads, we don't track you, and we never sell data.

Who runs Pausivo

Pausivo is run by Shidin, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For anything about privacy, write to shidin@shidin.com.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health data from Apple Health, or an advertising identifier.

Your habits are sensitive, and treated that way

Which habits you chose, your triggers and your other answers are used for one thing: running the app for you. They are sent only to Pausivo's own server. They are never put into crash reports, logs or any other company's service, and they are never shown on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, in widgets, notifications or on your Apple Watch. Those places show numbers at most (for example "Day 43").

The leaderboard

The weekly board shows nicknames and points, nothing else. Your nickname is public to other people who use Pausivo, so don't put your real name in it if you don't want it seen. You can change it, or hide yourself from the board with the "Show me on leaderboards" switch in Profile.

What stays on your phone

The photo you add to your "why" never leaves your iPhone: it is stored in the app's own folder, kept out of backups, and deleted when you sign out. Small files for the widgets and the Apple Watch (minutes, counts, a day number) stay on your devices. Your sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Pausivo has no advertising, no analytics that follow what you do, and no tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for anyone's marketing.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. You can delete it yourself at any time, in the app:

  • Profile → Settings → Delete all data removes your urges, tasks, points and Journey log, and keeps the account.
  • Profile → Delete account closes the account at once and removes everything belonging to it from our server within seven days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel Pausivo Plus in your Apple Account's settings. Apple and the other companies above keep their own records (for example of a purchase) under their own policies. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Write to shidin@shidin.com from the email address of your account and we will answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK) you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Pausivo is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. No system is perfectly safe; if something happens that affects your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. If the change is important, the app will say so.
