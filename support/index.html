Support

Write to shidin@shidin.com. A person reads it, usually within two working days. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you were doing.

How do I delete my account or my data?

In the app: Profile → Delete account closes the account and removes everything on our server within seven days. Profile → Settings → Delete all data clears your urges, tasks, points and Journey log but keeps the account. If you can't get into the app, write to us from your account's email address. A subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account: see below.

How do I cancel Pausivo Plus?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pausivo, or in the app Profile → Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are Apple's to give: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it

Open Profile → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. If Plus belongs to another Pausivo account, sign in to that one.

Pausivo Plus on a new phone

Sign in with the same Pausivo account. Plus follows the account. If it doesn't appear, use Restore purchases.

The Apple Watch app

Install Pausivo from the Watch app on your iPhone, then open Pausivo on the iPhone once while signed in: the watch signs in by itself. If it says "Finish on your iPhone", open the iPhone app with the watch nearby. The watch never shows which habit you chose.

Why is there an energy limit?

Every started task uses 10 energy. You start with 100 and get 30 back every morning. Pausivo Plus removes the limit.

Privacy questions

The privacy policy says what is stored and who is involved. For anything else, write to us.

Pausivo is not a medical service. If you're struggling, talk to a doctor or a local support line.
