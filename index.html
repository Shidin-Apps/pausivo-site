Urges come and go.

When one hits, Pausivo hands you a surprising 2–10 minute task, times it, and shows what you got out of it. No streaks, no guilt.

One tap from urge to task

Tap I have an urge. Pausivo picks one short task that fits where you are: a quick recipe, a stretch, a story. A timer keeps you company. Afterwards you see what you got: steps, grams of protein, something you now know.

Count what's changing

Journey keeps your day count, the money and time you kept, and your patterns: when, where and why urges show up. A slip is a note, not a reset.

Private by design

Your habit never shows on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, widgets or Apple Watch. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Read the privacy policy.
